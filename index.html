FLAME-GRILLED JUSTICE. DEEP-FRIED DEATH.

Twenty years ago, fast-food behemoths Ron's Burgers and General's Chicken decided that assassinating health-conscious TV chefs was just good business strategy. Fast forward through two decades of corporate escalation, and the high street has turned into an open war zone.

Recruits don tactical body armour, trade mortar fire for menu supremacy, and proudly lay down their lives for free extra-value meals for life.

Enter Marcus. He isn't a hero. He's just a four-star shift worker who went into the walk-in freezer in the early 2000s and got accidentally preserved at minus eighteen degrees. Thawed out into a smog-choked wasteland, Marcus is handed a spatulated rifle and drafted to Camp Burger.

ABOUT THE AUTHOR: S.R. MOORE

S.R. Moore is a literary titan and proud owner of at least three books. A decorated veteran of the fast-food trenches, Moore is a three-time McDonald's Employee of the Month winner—an honour that provided both invaluable first-hand research into corporate burger culture and a deep respect for the soft-serve machine.

Burger Wars is Moore's debut novel, written under the strict belief that customer service should always be taken to its absolute, chaotic extreme.
